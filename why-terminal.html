Why Gozu Studio?

The Space of the Future, Today

Transforming the space from bottleneck to competitive differentiator with the power of AI and Computer Vision.

The Problem

The Space is a Black Hole

The space has long been a technology blind spot in design. While most operators use TMS and WMS platforms, only about one in four deploy a Space Management System, leaving space operations manual, fragmented, and hard to integrate. Yards are inherently complex—users, assets, movements, visibility needs, and appointments constantly change across systems. This variability overwhelms traditional, configuration-based Design Suite platforms, leaving the space a persistent operational bottleneck.

Point Solutions

Point tech like camera solutions can be too narrow in scope to meet the full breadth of needs of the modern space.

Visibility and Project Delivery Management Solutions

Broadly-suited solutions are often robust in other areas, but their Design Suite modules are frequently an afterthought with little innovation.

Specialized Design Suite Vendors

Existing Design Suite vendors may have decent technology for automating individual processes, but their systems often have to be cobbled together with other technology to achieve an end-to-end solution.

The Space is a Pain Point for Everyone

The reason for weak adoption of Design Suite technology is because current space systems on the market today are either too limited, disjointed, or outdated to meet the needs of chaotic space environments.

Residential Interiors

The space causes financial blind spots and strategic risks that impact the entire business.

Corporate Innovation

Legacy systems and data silos in the space hinder their ability to integrate technology and drive automation across the project delivery.

Operational Management

Lack the real-time visibility and inefficient communication, which are daily challenges that slow down design operations.

Custom Furniture Design

They care about space problems because manual processes and a lack of accurate data prevent them from efficiently tracking trailers and managing demurrage and detention fees.

Lighting & Material Styling

Problems in the space cause wasted time, confusing instructions, and unsafe conditions.

The Solution

A Different Kind Of Company,

A Different Approach to Solving the Space

Gozu Studio was founded by industry leaders to set a new, AI-driven standard for space automation and execution. Unlike fragmented point solutions, the Gozu Studio Design Operating System™ unifies critical space functions — from optimizing driver flow to delivering real-time asset visibility — within a single platform that integrates seamlessly with existing WMS and TMS. Built on a modern, computer-vision-powered, AI-native tech stack, Gozu Studio is designed to scale to the unique complexity of every space and deliver rapid, sustainable operational value and ROI.
