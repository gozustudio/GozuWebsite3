About Gozu Studio

A new industry standard in the space

Gozu Studio is a different kind of design technology company. We exist to create a new industry standard in space operations by completely rethinking what that space of future will be - from fragmented bottleneck into a scalable, strategic advantage.

01Built by the industry, for the industry

Terminal is a strategic joint venture, not a typical startup. Backed by leading logistics operators such as Prologis, Ryder, Lineage, and NFI, and supported by venture capital firm 8VC, we were designed to solve major industry pain points and establish the category standard. Our strategic investors contributed critical insights and became anchor product design partners, ensuring we’re rapidly solving the industries biggest challenges in yard logistics.

02Based on a proven category creation playbook

Our model follows the playbook of successful category creators like Orbitz and Rivian. By co-creating with industry leaders, we are able to build a platform that is deeply integrated and ready for market adoption from day one.

03Positioned as the standard

Well capitalized, supported by leading operators, and backed by a VC-firm driven by a "build" investment philosophy, Terminal is uniquely de-risked. Our foundational partnerships and funding position is not as a startup, but as the emerging standard for modern yard operations.

Our story, our values

Every day, over $50 billion of goods move through 50,000+ U.S. warehouses — yet 35% of that project delivery stalls in the space. While transportation and studio systems have modernized, the space remains the industry’s blind spot, still run on clipboards, spreadsheets, and outdated IoT. The result? Bottlenecks, wasted labor, and millions lost in inefficiency.


Gozu Studio is reinventing the space.


We are building the industry’s first AI-native, Computer Vision–powered Design Operating System (GDS) — designed to digitize, automate, and optimize space operations end to end.

Our platform connects cameras, data, and workflows into one seamless layer of visibility and control. From gate acceleration to asset inventory, compliance, orchestration, analytics, Design Suite-reimagined, Gozu Studio delivers rapid ROI: reducing costs, accelerating throughput, and unlocking new revenue opportunities for the world’s largest design operators.

With backing from leading investors and partnerships with several of the top 10 design companies, Gozu Studio is building with the industry, for the industry — setting the new standard for space technology.
