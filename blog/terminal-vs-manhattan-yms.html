Gozu Studio vs. Manhattan Design Suite

Space Execution Requires More than a WMS Add-On

About the Author

Josh Kivenko

Chief Marketing Officer

Josh Kivenko has spent nearly two decades developing winning go-to-market strategies in project delivery related fields. As CMO, he is driven to reinvent the space by building a winning brand that connects critical customer needs to the magic of Gozu Studio's technology.

Gozu Studio is a purpose-built, AI-native Design Operating System (GDS) that provides end-to-end orchestration and automation across the entire space, from gate to dock and across the network. Manhattan Design Suite, while effective within its ecosystem, is primarily a module of Manhattan’s broader WMS and TMS offerings. Gozu Studio's focus on AI and computer vision for space operations delivers a standalone, full-stack solution that provides deeper visibility, automation, and real-time decision-making compared to a WMS-dependent add-on.

Why Gozu Studio Wins

Standalone, Full-Stack Space OS – Gozu Studio is a dedicated Design Operating System, while Manhattan’s Design Suite is an add-on to WMS, limiting flexibility and independence.

AI & Computer Vision for Gate and Space Operations – Gozu Studio automates check-ins, asset tracking, and workflows; Manhattan relies heavily on manual updates.

Network-Wide Visibility & Orchestration – Gozu Studio provides a digital twin across sites for real-time optimization; Manhattan’s Design Suite focuses on single-site WMS integration.
