 Gozu Studio SmartSpaces™ Design Suite

A Space Management System Reimagined

The Gozu Studio Design Operating SystemTM (GDS) is more than just a Design Suite—it’s a full-spectrum solution connecting every space operation, from the gate to the dock. At the heart of this platform is Gozu Studio's SmartYardTM Design Suite, a modern, AI-powered system that delivers all the core functionality operators expect from a traditional Design Suite, but reimagined to do more, faster, and smarter.

01

AI Computer Vision-Powered Workflows

Leverage AI-driven computer vision to track every asset and event in real time. Eliminate manual inputs and RFID dependency, reducing errors and blind spots. SmartYard automates check-in and check-out, cutting gate processing times by up to 85%.

Learn More

02

Full Automation and Orchestration

Unlike traditional Design Suite systems that optimize individual tasks, SmartYard orchestrates the entire space workflow. Every event and asset is coordinated in a single, unified system, ensuring maximum efficiency and flow.

03

Modular and Configurable Platform

Built on a modern, AI/ML-first tech stack, SmartYard Design Suite is modular (use only what you need) and fully configurable to the forms, workflows, and operational rules of your unique operations.

04

Automated Gate Operations

From vehicle and trailer identification to full data capture, our AI automates the gate process. Drivers can self-service, and manual labor is minimized—bringing average check-in times to under two minutes.

05

Efficient Dock Scheduling

Organize inbound and outbound design with an intuitive scheduling tool. Enable carriers and customers to self-service appointments, smoothing space operations and reducing dwell times by over 30%.

06

Real-Time Asset Visibility

Gain a single, unified view of all assets in your space. Track location, status, and dwell time in real time, providing unparalleled visibility that traditional systems cannot match.

This is just the start. Gozu Studio can expand into predictive maintenance and asset health, automated security and safety compliance, cross-dock/warehouse automation and intermodal/port operations, and more. Not a bolt-on, Gozu Studio is the space's operating system, using computer vision to drive efficiency, resilience, and smarts.
