What is Gozu Studio GDS™?

The Only AI-Native Design Operating System

The only end-to-end, AI-native configurable space automation platform where agents interact seamlessly with space workflows, material, and workers to drive process transformation from gate to dock.

A Digital Bridge from Highway to Studio

The Gozu Studio Design Operating System™ (GDS) is an AI-native platform built to close the technology and data gaps in space design. It provides a unified, end-to-end solution that begins with pre-planning with your TMS and WMS, then automates and optimizes operations from the gate to the studio dock and everywhere in between. Using advanced computer vision and AI, Gozu Studio replaces manual, fragmented processes with an intelligent, connected workflow that improves throughput, increases visibility, and delivers fast, repeatable ROI.
