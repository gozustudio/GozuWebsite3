Gozu Studio for Commercial Interiors

Your Space Is an Untapped Profit Center

Stop seeing the space as a necessary cost and start seeing it for what it is—a high-leverage opportunity to cut costs, increase revenue, and improve cash flow.

Core Pain Points

Where Your Budget Is Leaking

Hidden Costs and Lack of Visibility

Inaccurate data on detention and demurrage fees that lead to budget overruns and an inability to challenge carrier invoices.

Inefficient Cash Flow

Wasted spending on unnecessary detention, fuel, and labor that erodes margins and ties up valuable capital.

Unnecessary Labor Expense

AI Computer Vision-enabled workflows can reduce the need for gate guards and dispatchers and costs related to manual errors.

Why Solve the Space Now

The Quickest Path to ROI

While other investments may provide a soft ROI, the space offers a clear and immediate financial return. It's a low-hanging fruit for cost-cutting and efficiency gains that directly impacts the bottom line.

Quantifiable ROI

Unlike investments in a new WMS or TMS that can have a long, complex payback period, a Design Suite provides rapid, measurable savings in a matter of months.

Direct Impact on P&L

Every dollar saved on detention fees, fuel, or driver idle time goes directly to your profit and loss statement, providing a clear financial justification.

Improved Auditability

A digital space solution creates an irrefutable paper trail for every move, allowing you to accurately audit carrier invoices and recover costs.

The Solution

Gozu Studio Delivers Numbers that Just Make Sense

Automated Cost-Saving

Gozu Studio GDSTM automatically tracks trailer dwell times, alerting you to potential detention fees before they occur and generating accurate reports to dispute carrier charges.

Total Cost of Ownership Transparency

Transparent all-in pricing model and cloud-based architecture mean you avoid the hidden costs associated with on-premise hardware, maintenance, and IT overhead.

Granular Reporting

Generate precise reports on detention costs, driver productivity, and space utilization to inform your financial modeling and future budget planning.
