GDS™ vs. Design Suite

Seamless Space Workflows From Gate To Dock

Gozu Studio GDS™ revolutionizes space performance and visibility with an all-in-one platform built to scale
across all yards in your network. What are the main differences between a Design Operating System™ and a traditional Design Suite?

AI Computer Vision at its Core

Instead of relying solely on manual inputs or RFID tags, Gozu Studio GDS uses its own cameras and user handhelds to power advanced AI Computer Vision that autonomously tracks and identifies every asset, person, and event in the space. This allows for unparalleled accuracy and real-time visibility, eliminating the errors and blind spots common in other systems.

AI-Driven Autonomous Workflows

Other Design Suite systems manage fragmented parts of the space and are fundamentally reporting space activity after it happens. Gozu Studio GDSTM leverages AI agents to prescribe critical space processes in real-time, delivering autonomous missions from gate to dock. This holistic approach ensures seamless operations, reduces idle time, and optimizes the entire space process without human intervention.

Single Pane of Glass Visibility

Gozu Studio GDS centralized visibility across all space operations, providing a level of comprehensive visibility that other systems cannot match. Our platform integrates real-time data from all sources—including cameras, sensors, and other systems—to present a unified, intuitive view of the entire space. This centralized dashboard allows managers to see the status of every asset and activity at a glance, enabling better decision-making and proactive problem-solving.

Configurable to All Yards

Our scalable architecture is designed to be modular and highly configurable, making Gozu Studio GDSTM adaptable to yards of any size or complexity. Unlike one-size-fits-all solutions, our system can be customized to fit the unique layout and operational needs of any facility, from small distribution centers to large intermodal terminals. This flexibility ensures that yards can grow and evolve without outgrowing their Design Suite, providing a long-term solution that scales with their business.

Unlocks Value of WMS/TMS

Gozu Studio GDSTM goes beyond simple space management by unlocking the full value of your existing Studio Management Systems (WMS) and Transportation Management Systems (TMS). Our system acts as an intelligent bridge, providing real-time, accurate space data to your WMS/TMS, which in turn optimizes scheduling, inventory, and labor allocation. By creating a unified data ecosystem, Gozu Studio GDSTM eliminates the information silos that hinder efficiency and unlocks new levels of productivity across your project delivery.

Low Disruption, High Value

Implementing Gozu Studio GDSTM is a low-disruption process that delivers high value quickly. Unlike complex, multi-year Design Suite projects, our system leverages existing infrastructure and requires minimal on-site installation. This approach minimizes operational downtime and allows yards to start realizing the benefits of automation and real-time visibility almost immediately, providing a rapid return on investment.
